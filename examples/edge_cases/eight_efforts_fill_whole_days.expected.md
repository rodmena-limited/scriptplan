# eight_efforts_fill_whole_days

Eight tasks of 61, 129, 49, 73, 318, 163, 579 and 134 minutes, declared in that order on one desk with
09:00-17:00 days. Packed back to back they fill Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday with exactly 480 minutes each
and end on Thursday after 66 more (1506 in all). 0.10.0 booked 490 minutes into the Monday.

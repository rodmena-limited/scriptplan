# shared_slot_forward_60min

Three tasks of 40, 13 and 40 minutes on one desk, declared in that order, same priority,
60-minute slots. One person works one task at a time, so they run back to back from 09:00:
t0 09:00 + 40 = 09:40; t1 09:40 + 13 = 09:53; t2 09:53 + 40 = 10:33. Total 93 minutes.
0.10.0 started all three at 09:00 at 60-minute resolution (issuedb #11).

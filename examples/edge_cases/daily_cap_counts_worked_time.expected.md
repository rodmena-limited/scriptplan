# daily_cap_counts_worked_time

dailymax 60min on the desk. Monday: t0 40 + t1 13 = 53 minutes, leaving 7 under the cap, so t2 works
09:53-10:00 on Monday and its other 33 minutes on Tuesday, 09:00-09:33. The cap blocks at exactly 60
minutes and releases on the next day. 0.10.0 counted whole booked hours against the cap and spread the
tasks over Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday.
